Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026 · TINKL. — Every Potty. Perfectly Tracked.

TINKL. is an offline-first pet care journal. It works without an account, serves no ads, and never sells your data. This policy explains what is stored, what is synced, and the controls you have.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — pet profiles, potty/activity logs, medications, weights, vaccinations, notes, reminders, and app settings — is stored locally on your device in its built-in database (IndexedDB / localStorage). It never leaves your device unless you turn on Cloud Backup.

2. Cloud Backup (opt-in)

Cloud Backup is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, the app uploads an encrypted-in-transit snapshot of your local data to our sync service at tinkl-sync.techguruofficial.workers.dev, where it is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Durable Objects). Cloudflare may store this data in a region outside your own. The snapshot is protected by a random sync credential generated on your device. Our server stores your snapshot in an isolated location derived from a one-way hash of that credential — the credential itself is never sent to be stored, and there is no account, name, or email attached to your backup. Your recovery code (Settings → Cloud Sync → Recovery Code) is the only way to reach your backup from another device; we cannot reset it for you, and anyone who has the code can read the backup, so treat it like a password.

  • Back up: Settings → Cloud Sync → Back Up to Cloud
  • Restore: Settings → Cloud Sync → Restore from Cloud
  • Delete: Settings → Cloud Sync → Delete Cloud Backup — permanently removes your snapshot from our servers

3. Push notifications (opt-in)

If you enable push notifications in Settings, your device's push subscription (endpoint and encryption keys, no pet data) is stored in our push registry so reminders can reach you even when the app is closed. Disabling notifications removes the subscription.

4. Vet Pass sharing

Vet Pass reports are encoded directly into the share link itself and decoded on the recipient's device. Report contents are never uploaded to our servers as part of sharing.

5. What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.
  • Diagnostic data is limited to what your device and our infrastructure providers log automatically (e.g. standard request logs).

6. Children

TINKL. is intended for pet owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
