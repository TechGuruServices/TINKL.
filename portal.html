Clinic Portal — Pilot

For veterinary clinics · TINKL. — Every Potty. Perfectly Tracked.

TINKL. is building a simple portal for veterinary clinics. It isn't ready yet. This page says plainly what it will do — and how your clinic can join the free pilot that shapes it.

What your clinic will get

  • Clean weekly health logs — potty patterns, weights, medications, symptoms and visits from each pet owner, structured and in one place, instead of pieced together at intake.
  • Vet Pass reports — a pet owner shares a read-only summary link with your clinic. No sign-in needed to read it.
  • Faster intake, less lookup time — the history is already organized when the patient walks in.

How sharing works

Pet owners choose what to share, and only with the clinic they pick. Nothing is shared automatically, and your clinic never sees data an owner didn't send. By default, each pet's logs stay on the owner's own device.

The pilot

Free. No payment, no contract, and no login yet. Pilot clinics talk to us directly and tell us what the portal needs to do. When the portal opens, pilot clinics are first in.
